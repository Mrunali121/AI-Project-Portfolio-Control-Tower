          How the health score works.

          Schedule 30% (SPI) + Cost 30% (CPI) + Risk 15% (open risk exposure) + Issues 15% (open issues by severity) + Resources 10% (share of the team that is over-allocated). Green 75+, Amber 55-74, Red below 55. A project is escalated when it is Red, SPI < 0.80, CPI < 0.85, forecast slip > 30 days, or a critical issue is open.
